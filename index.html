FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is iDrinkALot?

iDrinkALot is a drink tracker for nights out. You log drinks as you go and the app shows your estimated BAC live, your sober-by time, units, calories and session goals. You can also run group sessions where friends see each other's drinks in real time.

How does iDrinkALot estimate BAC?

It uses the Widmark formula with your weight, sex and age, plus a stomach fullness slider that changes how fast alcohol is absorbed. The curve is recalculated every second. BAC numbers are estimates, not medical measurements. Don't use them to decide if you can drive.

Is iDrinkALot available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. On iPhone, open app.idrinkalot.com in Safari and add it to your Home Screen, no App Store needed. On Android, join the Google Play beta above, or use the same web app in your browser.

Can I track drinks with friends?

Yes. Start a group session, pick a bar and invite friends. Everyone sees each other's drink count, units and BAC estimate live, and you can tap any member to see their full curve and timeline. Add friends by username, eight-character friend code or QR code.

Does it show promille or BAC percent?

Both. Switch between promille (‰) and BAC % in the settings.

Who can see my drinking data?

Only you and the friends you add. There are no public profiles and no algorithmic feed. We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers, and deleting your account removes everything. See the privacy policy.
